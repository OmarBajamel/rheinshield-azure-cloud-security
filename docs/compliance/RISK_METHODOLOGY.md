# Risk methodology

Likelihood and impact use 1-5 ordinal scales. Inherent score is likelihood × impact before planned treatment; residual score is reassessed after the design control. Scores 1-5 are Low, 6-11 Medium, 12-19 High and 20-25 Critical. Impact considers safety, service availability, confidentiality/integrity, financial, contractual and regulatory consequences.

RheinCommerce's fictional appetite is Low for restricted-data disclosure, privileged compromise, untested critical recovery and late material-incident reporting; Medium for reversible non-production disruption. Critical/High risk requires an accountable executive owner and dated mitigation. Avoid, mitigate, transfer or accept are valid treatments; acceptance requires explicit evidence and review at least quarterly.

The register is scenario analysis. Residual values express a reasoned target and are not claims of measured risk reduction.
