# BSI IT-Grundschutz mapping

The source register records the BSI's currently listed IT-Grundschutz-Kompendium Edition 2023 as of retrieval. RheinShield cross-references relevant module families including ISMS.1, ORP.1/3/4, CON.1/3/6/8, OPS.1.1.x, OPS.2.3, NET.1.1, DER.1, DER.2.1 and DER.4. Retired module `OPS.2.1` and Kubernetes-specific `APP.4.4` are intentionally excluded because they do not describe this lab. Exact requirement selection and protection-needs analysis would be refined for a real information domain.

The asset inventory, BIA, role model, policy baseline, evidence collector, incident exercise and German operating documents supply inspectable implementation references. The mapping is educational and does not represent BSI certification or a completed Grundschutz check.
