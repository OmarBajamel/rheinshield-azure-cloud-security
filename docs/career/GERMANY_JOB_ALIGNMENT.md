# Germany job alignment

Market snapshot: **2026-08-29**  
Sample: **15 current vacancies**: 14 original employer pages and one official recruiter page retained for a highly relevant Azure landing-zone role.  
Purpose: shape RheinShield's demonstrable evidence and later CV/interview language. This is a directional sample, not a statistical survey of the whole German market.

## What the sample says

The strongest positioning is not “Azure lab builder”. Employers repeatedly ask for candidates who can connect four forms of work:

1. build and operate Microsoft cloud controls;
2. automate delivery with IaC, source control and pipelines;
3. investigate security events and communicate the result; and
4. translate ISO, BSI and NIS-2 expectations into owned, auditable measures.

RheinShield is shaped for that intersection, and the released paths in the evidence matrix point to completed, inspectable artifacts. Each claim remains bounded by its explicit validation status; plan and fixture evidence are not presented as production experience.

## Recruiter-facing proof hierarchy

Use this order in README, dashboard, CV and interviews:

1. **Tested code:** Terraform/Bicep, policies, detections, queries, workflows and tests.
2. **Traceable result:** an evidence manifest ties each claim to a file, test run, timestamp and capability status.
3. **Operational narrative:** INC-001 shows triage, investigation, response, reporting and lessons learned.
4. **Governance narrative:** the same controls connect to risks, BSIG themes, ISO identifiers and BSI modules.
5. **Stakeholder narrative:** bilingual management and technical summaries explain decisions, limitations and next actions.

Screenshots support this proof hierarchy but do not replace it.

## CV and interview wording guardrails

- Say “implemented and fixture-tested” only after the tests pass.
- Say “plan-validated” for a successful Terraform validation/plan without an apply.
- Say “live-validated” only when the exact cloud capability was deployed and tested, with sanitized evidence.
- Prefer exact counts generated from the repository at release time; do not freeze aspirational counts from the master brief into the CV.
- Do not say “made a company NIS2-compliant”, “certified”, “prevented attacks” or “reduced production risk”.
- A defensible positioning sentence is: “Built a portfolio case study that connects Azure security engineering, detection-as-code and evidence-oriented NIS-2/ISO/BSI mapping using synthetic data.”

## Practical application priorities

1. Prioritize junior SOC/DevSecOps and junior Microsoft-cloud/IAM roles where the sample explicitly accepts 1–2 years or a development path.
2. Treat C1/C2 German requirements as real screening criteria; the German dashboard and interview story should sound natural, but do not claim a CEFR level unless independently supported.
3. Use senior roles to define the growth roadmap, not to mislabel project work as senior professional experience.
4. Consider SC-200, AZ-500, SC-300 and Terraform certification signals only as optional follow-up; tested portfolio evidence remains the immediate priority.
5. Check citizenship/security-clearance constraints role by role. For example, NVISO's posting includes a NATO-member-state/Austrian citizenship condition; candidate eligibility is not assumed here.
