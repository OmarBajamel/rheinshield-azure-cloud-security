4. **SOC (60s):** walk through the 14-rule catalog, benign/malicious test matrix, five hunts, three workbooks, and dry-run SOAR.
5. **Incident (45s):** explain the INC-001 correlation and exercise-only MTTD/MTTA/MTTR.
6. **Risk/compliance (50s):** show conditional German NIS2 applicability, 27 risks, 20 controls, and non-certification disclaimer.
7. **Methodology (40s):** close with deterministic telemetry, sanitization, hashes, browser/accessibility tests, and the live-Azure limitation.
