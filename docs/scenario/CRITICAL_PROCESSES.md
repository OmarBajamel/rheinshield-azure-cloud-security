# Critical processes

1. Authenticate customer, supplier, and workforce identities.
2. Accept, validate, and persist marketplace orders.
3. Exchange fulfilment status with suppliers.
4. Resolve customer incidents without excessive data exposure.
5. Detect, assess, contain, recover, and report cybersecurity incidents.
6. Restore platform data and configuration from tested backups.
7. Approve and deliver infrastructure changes through OIDC-protected CI/CD.
8. Review privileged, guest, and workload-identity access.

Trust boundaries exist at the public edge, workload network, platform services, identity tenant, supplier integrations, CI/CD control plane, SOC workspace, and evidence publication boundary.
