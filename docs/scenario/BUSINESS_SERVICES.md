# Business services

| Service | Customers | Availability target | Data | Security objective |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| B2B Marketplace | Buyer/supplier organizations | 99.9% | accounts, catalogue, orders | prevent account takeover and unauthorized changes |
| Order Processing | Operations and suppliers | 99.95% | orders, payment references | preserve integrity and traceability |
| Supplier Integration | Partner systems | 99.5% | supplier, catalogue, fulfilment | authenticate partners and contain compromise |
| Customer Support | Business customers | 99.5% | tickets, account context | least privilege and privacy |
| Identity Platform | All staff/workloads | 99.99% | identity and access metadata | strong authentication and governed privilege |
| Security Operations | Management and responders | 99.9% | logs, alerts, evidence | detect, investigate and report reliably |

Service owners maintain recovery evidence and accept residual risk; technical owners operate the controls.
