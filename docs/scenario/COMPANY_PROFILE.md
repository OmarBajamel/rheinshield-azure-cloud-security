# RheinCommerce GmbH - fictional company profile

RheinCommerce GmbH is a clearly fictional educational case study and is not a customer or employer. It is headquartered in Düsseldorf, employs about 280 people, trades above the relevant medium-enterprise turnover threshold, and operates across Germany and other EU countries. Its B2B marketplace, order-processing, supplier-integration, and customer-support services depend on continuous online availability while a legacy platform migrates to Microsoft Azure.

The target state separates development, test, and production, centralizes identity/governance/monitoring, and makes evidence traceable. The public repository contains only synthetic data. The legal-scope assessment is conditional and is not legal advice.
