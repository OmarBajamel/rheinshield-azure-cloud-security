# Visual asset provenance

All dashboard screenshots, architecture diagrams, social cards, carousel slides, and QR assets were generated specifically for RheinShield from project-owned source and synthetic data.

`public/og.png` originated from one built-in OpenAI image-generation request. The prompt concept was: **an abstract, professional Azure cloud-security command center for a German digital marketplace, using navy, mint, and restrained blue geometry with shield, cloud, network, and evidence-flow motifs; no logos, people, customer data, text, identifiers, or screenshots**. The result was cropped, optimized, and metadata-reviewed by the local media generator before inclusion. No third-party stock image was used.

The remaining raster assets were rendered locally by `tools/visual-evidence/` and `tools/career-assets/` from project code, diagrams, and screenshots. Dependency licenses are inventoried in `artifacts/evidence/license-inventory.json`; the repository's original content is released under the root `LICENSE`.
