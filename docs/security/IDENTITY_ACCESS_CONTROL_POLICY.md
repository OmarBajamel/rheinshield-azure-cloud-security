# Identity and access-control policy

Access is group-based, least-privileged, sponsor-owned, and periodically reviewed. Privileged roles are eligible and time-bound where PIM licensing exists; emergency identities are separate, excluded from Conditional Access lockout, and monitored. Workloads use managed identity or constrained OIDC. Evidence: `identity/`, `docs/identity/RBAC_MATRIX.md`, and RS001–RS006.
