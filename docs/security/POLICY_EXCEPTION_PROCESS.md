# Policy exception process

1. The requester records control ID, resource, business justification, owner, compensating controls, risk rating, and expiry.
2. Cloud Governance and Information Security validate the smallest possible scope; data protection and service owners join when relevant.
3. Approved exemptions have a maximum 90-day lifetime, a named treatment action, and monitoring.
4. Evidence is linked in the control matrix. Expired exemptions alert the owner and become non-compliant.
5. Emergency exceptions are retrospective-review items within two business days.

Exceptions do not modify the baseline definition. They are assignments scoped to explicit project resources; broad or permanent exemptions require architecture and risk acceptance review.
