# Privileged access runbook

1. Confirm the change ticket, target project scope, backup/rollback, and requester identity.
2. Activate the smallest eligible role for no more than four hours with MFA, approval and justification.
3. Verify the effective scope excludes unrelated subscriptions and tenant hierarchy.
4. Perform and log the change; never share or export tokens.
5. Validate the expected control/evidence state and roll back on unexpected impact.
6. End activation, review audit logs, and attach sanitized evidence.

Emergency access requires a declared incident, dual control where available, immediate monitoring, post-use credential rotation, and retrospective review within two business days.
